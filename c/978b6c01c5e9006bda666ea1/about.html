How we work with clients.

  • As part of your team

    We operate as an extension of your staff, not a vendor you reach through a support inbox. You know who is working your claims, and you can call them.

  • Ahead of problems

    Submitting claims is the minimum. We look for underpayments, fix the causes of preventable denials, and move cash in faster.

  • In plain view

    Leadership sees where revenue stands at any point. When something is wrong, we say so and explain what we are doing about it.

Leadership

Abe Mandelbaum

Co-Founder and Chief Executive Officer

Abe is a healthcare entrepreneur with experience across healthcare operations, revenue cycle management, insurance eligibility and healthcare technology. He has founded and led companies built to simplify complicated healthcare processes, working closely with providers to improve their financial and operational results.

At Clearstream he leads strategy, business development and growth. He is also co-founder of Sella Health, a healthcare technology company that makes insurance eligibility information easier for providers to access and act on.

Yechiel (Kyle) Kahan

Co-Founder and Chief Financial Officer

Before Clearstream, Kyle spent more than six years in real estate finance underwriting, contributing to over $2.5 billion in financing transactions. That work, evaluating complex financial structures and analyzing risk, shapes how he runs Clearstream's finances.

As CFO he oversees financial operations, planning and long-term objectives, with a data-driven focus on efficient systems and sustainable growth.

Gitti Kahan

Co-Founder

Gitti brings nearly 15 years of experience in healthcare administration, insurance contracting, provider credentialing and revenue cycle management. She started in skilled nursing, specializing in contracting, credentialing and billing, before expanding to serve a wider range of providers.

In 2020 she founded Clearstream Advisers, a consulting firm focused on insurance contracting, credentialing and payer negotiations. In 2022 she co-founded Clearstream Billing Solutions to bring that payer experience to revenue cycle management for behavioral health and substance use disorder providers.
