Verification of benefits

Payment problems often start before the patient is admitted. A plan can show as active and still carve out behavioral health, limit days at a given level of care, or require authorization before the first day.

We verify the behavioral health benefit specifically, so admissions and finance know what is covered and what the patient will owe before the facility relies on it.

Authorizations and utilization review

Our UR team is led by licensed clinicians. They understand clinical documentation, medical necessity and the continued-stay criteria insurers apply, and they make the case for the level of care the patient needs.

  • Initial authorizations
  • Concurrent reviews
  • Tracking every authorization against the stay
  • Coordination with your clinical team
  • Peer-to-peer review support

The result is fewer authorization-related denials and less time your clinicians spend on the phone with payers.

Coding and claims

PHP and IOP are billed differently from routine outpatient care, and the details decide whether a claim is paid: daily per diem billing, CPT and HCPCS codes, revenue codes, and what happens when a patient steps between levels of care.

We build and submit claims to each payer's rules, and we check them before they go out. An EMR can produce a claim; it takes a trained team and a defined workflow to make sure it is the right one.

Payment posting

Every payment is posted back to its claim and compared with what the payer should have paid. Underpayments are identified and pursued, not absorbed.

Denials and appeals

Each denial is worked to a decision, with an appeal where one is warranted. We also trace denials back to their cause, whether that is a missed authorization, a coding issue or a documentation gap, so the same problem stops recurring.

A/R recovery

We work outstanding receivables to resolution, including aged balances from before we started. For many new clients this is where the first recovered revenue comes from.

Reporting

Leadership gets a clear picture of financial performance: what has been billed, collected, denied and is still outstanding, and where the problems are. You should not have to ask your billing company how things are going.

Medicare, Medicaid and commercial plans.

Each payer type has its own rules, reimbursement methods and reasons for denying a claim. We bill all three, and we keep programs compliant while collecting everything they are owed.

Medicare

Coverage requirements, provider eligibility, documentation standards, and the regulations that govern PHP, IOP, opioid treatment services and other covered behavioral health care.

Medicaid

State-specific regulations, managed care organization requirements, authorization processes and reimbursement structures.

Commercial

Payer-specific contracts, medical necessity guidelines, authorization requirements, coding policies and reimbursement practices.

MAT providers, methadone clinics and Opioid Treatment Programs

OTP reimbursement differs sharply from standard outpatient behavioral health billing. Medicare OTP billing, Medicaid reimbursement, bundled payment methods, HCPCS coding, medication administration and counseling services each have rules of their own.

We handle that billing accurately and work the denials and underpayments specific to this treatment model.
