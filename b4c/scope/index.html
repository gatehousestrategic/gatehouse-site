Every leaderboard page

  • Full ranked list with photo, team and the honor's number, searchable by name or city
  • Filters by team and by season, so past years stay viewable
  • A plain explanation of how the honor is counted
  • A Sponsor button on every row

Leaderboards inside pages

  • Rider page: the rider's own donor leaderboard, first believer marked, plus the rider's rank on each honor
  • Team page: the team's riders ranked on any of the eight honors, plus the team's donor leaderboard
  • Teams page: teams ranked by dollars, donors per rider, riders at goal and new donors

Leaders update within a minute of each gift. Ties go to whoever reached the number first.

05 · New

Donations and
sponsor-a-rider

The new form keeps every option the current one has, and adds giving to several riders in one checkout.

Sponsor a rider

  • A Sponsor button on every rider page and leaderboard row opens the form with that rider chosen
  • One gift can be split across several riders, with one payment and one receipt
  • A donor can sponsor a whole team
  • Monthly giving until ride day, each payment credited to the same rider

Corporate sponsorship online

  • Each sponsorship item ($7.5K to $250K) shows live SOLD status
  • A company reserves an item online and pays by card or asks for an invoice
  • The logo appears in its tier once payment clears

Counting rules for every honor

06 · New

Admin, data and
integrations

Staff run the event without a developer, and the history back to 2010 comes across so the honors work in year one.

Admin tools

  • Riders and teams: approve, edit, merge duplicates, move riders, set captains
  • Donations: enter checks and pledges, refund, reassign, mark self or household, link a match to its gift
  • Donors: merge duplicates, mark companies, see full history
  • Honors: preview boards, hide a rider, override one gift's counting with a note
  • Sponsorships, content and galleries edited by staff
  • Spreadsheet exports for the Chai Lifeline finance team

07 · Plan

Phasing

The site goes live before 2027 registration opens, so riders see their streak and history on day one.

  1. Export the data from Neon Fundraise and review the history
  2. Donation form, rider pages, team pages and riders list, matching today's site
  3. Honors, leaderboards and homepage leaders
  4. Registration, rider dashboard and admin tools
  5. Corporate sponsorship checkout and split gifts
  6. Test with last year's data, then switch the domain before registration opens
