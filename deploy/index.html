When the Right Tool Doesn't Exist, We Build It

Alongside client work, we look for gaps in a market, places where nothing on offer does the whole job, and build the product that fills them.

Event software

Soventro

The gap: the big event platforms cover everything but are notoriously clunky to use. The easier ones skip operational necessities like hotel room blocks, or handle them badly. Soventro runs the whole operation and stays simple to use.

Soventro is the event operating system: event website, registration, payments and invoices, agenda and speakers, attendee emails, hotel room blocks, onsite check-in and reporting. Attendees get their own app for the agenda, their schedule and their check-in code. Built-in AI handles the tedious parts. It predicts where each hotel block will land by its cutoff and suggests when to release rooms or ask for more, before attrition costs you. It matches events to the right venues. And it fits every speaker and attendee photo to the same frame, filling in the background without changing the person, so photos line up evenly on the event website and in the app.

Questions We Hear Most

Do we need an AI strategy if we just need an app built?

No. Plenty of engagements are pure build: you know what tool you need, we build it. Strategy work only comes in when the question is bigger than one piece of software.

How long does a typical build take?

Most custom tools ship in 6 to 12 weeks depending on scope. We scope tightly on purpose, so you get something usable fast rather than waiting a year for a perfect system.

Do you work with our existing team, or replace them?

We work alongside your team. On the build side we hand off clean, documented software. On the strategy side we help you hire and structure an AI team if you don't have one yet.

What if we don't know what we need yet?

That's normal, and it's exactly what the first conversation is for. We'll help you figure out whether the real issue is a missing tool or a missing strategy before we scope anything.

Can we do both a custom build and a strategy engagement?

Yes, and most clients end up doing both. They often start on one side and realize the other is needed to make the first investment pay off.
